The plan for today

  • See what actually happens between your browser and a web server
  • Get comfortable with the tools that let you watch and tamper with that traffic
  • Learn the big three bug families: injection, XSS, and broken access control
  • Hack 8 purpose-built vulnerable apps and grab their flags
Rules of engagement These labs exist to be attacked, so go wild. Real systems are different: if you don't own it or don't have written permission to test it, don't touch it. Unauthorized testing is illegal. Stick to labs, CTFs, and bug bounty programs that invite you in.
